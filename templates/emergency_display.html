Emergency

Dr. Health

Put this card on the home screen

Once it is an icon of its own, anyone can open it in an emergency without unlocking Dr. Health. Works on Android and iPhone.

Android — Chrome or Edge

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top-right.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. The new red-cross icon opens this card.

Android — Samsung Internet

  1. Tap the ☰ menu, then Add page to → Home screen.

Android — Firefox

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install or Add to Home screen.

iPhone or iPad — Safari

  1. Not in Private Browsing. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow).
  2. Add to Home Screen → Add. Use Safari; Chrome on iPhone cannot install it.

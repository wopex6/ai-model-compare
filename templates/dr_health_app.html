Tap for install instructions
  1. Open in real Safari: if you came from Messages/ WhatsApp/ Mail, tap the box-with-arrow at the top-right and choose Safari (not just this preview).
  2. Make sure you are not in Private Browsing — “Add to Home Screen” is hidden there.
  3. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow) in Safari’s bottom toolbar. If it’s hidden, scroll up slightly.
  4. Scroll the share sheet down to the Actions section. If you still can’t see Add to Home Screen, tap Edit Actions… at the bottom and turn it on.
  5. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
